HOW TO USE
Upload this whole folder to Cloudflare Pages. index.html must stay at the top level.

SHARED PARTS
The header and footer live in js/layout.js. Change them there once and every page updates. The services list is also there.

LOGO AND FAVICON
Save your JPEG as images/logo.jpg. It becomes the small circle next to your name and the browser tab icon.

IMAGES
Save photos in the images folder using the exact names below (.jpg, .png, .webp or .jpeg). Until a photo is added, its shape shows a description of what belongs there.
