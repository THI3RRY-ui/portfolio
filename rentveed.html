RentVeed

Hunting for a rental in Buea and Molyko means trekking in the sun, haggling with predatory agents and risking extortion. RentVeed lets you tour the house from your phone.

Visit rentveed.com
Role Founder and Lead Developer
Team and time Team of 5, November 2025 to present
Result Helping Students and young professionals find rentals in Buea and Molyko, Cameroon, without the stress of trekking in the sun and haggling with predatory agents.

What it does

  • Video-first listings with search and filters, so you tour a house from your phone
  • Verified listings, with Coins and an Access Pass paid through NotchPay (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money, cards)
  • A Hire section where plumbers, electricians and painters list their work
  • Referral program and a WhatsApp community

Built with

Flutter Dart Supabase Cloudflare Firebase Brevo NotchPay MySQL +More Frameworks
